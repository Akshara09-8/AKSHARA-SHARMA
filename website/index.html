ActiveLabel — Behaviour Annotation Optimization

ProblemBehavioural video datasets need expensive expert annotations, which makes large-scale model training hard.

SolutionAn active-learning pipeline comparing random, uncertainty-based and diversity-based sampling to find the most informative 5% of video samples to label, and measuring how each choice affects model accuracy.

Group Dynamics — Multi-Person Behaviour Analysis

ProblemHuman-robot interaction systems struggle with group-level social dynamics: speaker dominance, disengagement and interpersonal attention.

SolutionA multi-person behaviour analysis system that uses gaze direction and body orientation to identify dominant speakers and disengaged participants, aimed at socially aware human-robot interaction.
